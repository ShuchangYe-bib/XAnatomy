The severity of cardiomegaly is categorized by the degree to which the heart fills the thoracic cavity, with higher ratios indicating more significant disease: 
Normal: CTR 0.42–0.50.
Borderline/Mild: CTR > 0.50 (up to 0.55 in some definitions).
Moderate to Severe: CTR > 0.55–0.60, indicating clear, significant enlargement.
Severe/Extreme: CTR > 0.80 is rare, associated with advanced disease, profound cardiac remodeling, or severe congestive heart failure.
